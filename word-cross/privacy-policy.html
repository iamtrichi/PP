Privacy Policy — Word Cross

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Word Cross ("we", "our", or "the app") is a fully offline word-puzzle game. This privacy policy explains what data is (and is not) collected when you use the Word Cross mobile application, and how that data is handled.

2. Data Stored on Your Device Only (Local Storage)

Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not sync, upload, or share this data with any server. Data is persisted using the device's native storage (Capacitor Preferences, with a local-storage fallback) and remains entirely under your control:

  • Completed levels and games played
  • High scores and best times
  • Hint points and energy balance (with its last-refill timestamp)
  • App settings: sound on/off and volume, light/dark theme, selected language
  • Your ad-consent choice and whether the introduction tour has been shown

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials — the app has no sign-up or login
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Gameplay analytics or behavioural tracking
  • Any data you enter into the app (there is none to enter)

4. Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is used exclusively for the ad requests and version check described below. Word Cross does not request notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display a banner, and interstitial and rewarded video ads (the rewarded ads grant optional in-game hint points or energy). AdMob may collect anonymous information about your device and ad interactions in accordance with Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video completion)

On first launch, Google's User Messaging Platform may present a consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required). If you decline personalized ads, ad requests are made in non-personalized mode. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Version Check

When the app starts, it fetches a small version.json file from GitHub Pages (version.json) to determine whether a newer app version is available. This request carries no personal data — it only reads the published version numbers and compares them with the version string reported by the operating system. If a newer version exists, the app may show an optional update prompt, or (for unsupported versions) require an update and offer to open the app's Google Play listing. If the request fails (offline, timeout), the app simply continues and you can play normally.

7. First-Launch Language Detection

On the very first launch, and only when no language has been chosen yet, the app may call ipwho.is (with a 5-second timeout) to read the two-letter country code associated with your connection. It is used for one purpose only: to preselect the interface language (Arabic for Arabic-speaking countries; otherwise your device language is used). Nothing is stored on our servers, and the request is skipped entirely on later launches once a language is stored. You can change the language at any time inside the app.

8. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party libraries and platforms:

  • Capacitor (by Ionic) — native runtime; no data collected
  • Ionic — UI framework; no data collected
  • i18next — localization; no data collected
  • Google AdMob — see section 5 above
  • ipwho.is — see section 7 above

9. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any personal information to third parties. AdMob data is processed under Google's own privacy terms.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control:

  • Delete all app data: go to your device Settings → Apps → Word Cross → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes all locally stored progress. No data remains anywhere.

12. Security

Your game progress never leaves your device. The app has no backend database. The only external communications are: (a) anonymous AdMob ad requests, (b) the version-check JSON fetch from GitHub Pages, and (c) the one-time first-launch language lookup described in section 7 — none of which involve personal data.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
