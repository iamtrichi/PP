Privacy Policy — FlexiCalc

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

FlexiCalc ("we", "our", or "the app") is a smart unit converter and calculator for Android. Calculations and conversions run entirely on your device; the only network use is ad requests, as described in section 5 below. This privacy policy explains what data is (and is not) collected when you use the FlexiCalc mobile application, and how that data is handled.

2. Data Stored on Your Device Only (Local Storage)

Your calculation history and preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not sync, upload, or share this data with any server. Data is persisted using the device's native storage (Capacitor Preferences, with a local-storage fallback) and remains entirely under your control:

  • Calculation history (expressions, results, and converted values)
  • Calculator preferences: selected category and units
  • App settings: selected language
  • Your ad-consent choice

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials — the app has no sign-up or login
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking

4. Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is used exclusively for the ad requests described below. FlexiCalc does not request notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display a banner and rewarded video ads (watching a rewarded ad unlocks the history-export feature). AdMob may collect anonymous information about your device and ad interactions in accordance with Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video completion)

On first launch, Google's User Messaging Platform may present a consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required). If you decline personalized ads, ad requests are made in non-personalized mode. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party libraries and platforms:

  • Capacitor (by Ionic) — native runtime; no data collected
  • Ionic — UI framework; no data collected
  • i18next — localization; no data collected
  • Google AdMob — see section 5 above

7. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any personal information to third parties. AdMob data is processed under Google's own privacy terms.

8. Children's Privacy

FlexiCalc may be used by users of all ages, including children. The app is designed to be safe for general audiences: it has no sign-up or login, no chat or user-generated content, no in-app purchases, and no usage analytics. Rewarded ads are always optional and are never required to use the calculator.

Advertising shown to children is handled under Google's Families and child-directed policies: ad requests use Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow, and non-personalized ads mode is used wherever personalized advertising is not permitted. Parents can further limit ad personalization in the device settings (Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or reset the Advertising ID).

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control:

  • Delete all app data: go to your device Settings → Apps → FlexiCalc → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes all locally stored history. No data remains anywhere.

10. Security

Your calculation history never leaves your device. The app has no backend database. The only external communication is AdMob ad requests. These network requests necessarily transmit technically-required data (e.g. the Advertising ID and IP address for ads) as detailed in section 5; your calculations themselves never leave your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
