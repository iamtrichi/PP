Privacy Policy — GTA Cheats Guide

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Overview

GTA Cheats Guide ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline reference guide for cheat codes across nine Grand Theft Auto titles. The guide content is bundled with the app and works without an internet connection. Network access is used only for advertising and ad consent, as described in this policy.

This privacy policy explains what data is (and is not) collected when you use the GTA Cheats Guide Android application (package com.trichi.gta.cheat), and how that data is handled.

GTA Cheats Guide is an unofficial fan-made guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. All game names, trademarks, logos, and related assets are the property of their respective owners.

2. Data Stored on Your Device Only

The app stores a small amount of preference data locally on your device only. We do not sync, upload, or share this data with any server. Data is persisted using the device's native storage (Capacitor Preferences, with a local-storage fallback) and remains under your control:

  • Your selected interface language
  • Your ad-consent choice (personalized or non-personalized)
  • Whether the introduction/walkthrough has already been shown

Search terms are processed in memory to filter the bundled cheat list and are not saved. The app has no account system and does not store a browsing or cheat-viewing history. Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following ourselves:

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account credentials — the app has no sign-up or login
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Search queries, cheat-viewing history, or other usage history
  • Payment or financial information
  • User-generated content — the app does not accept user content

The app does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Advertising-related data processed by Google AdMob is described in section 5 below.

4. Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions for ad requests and ad-consent handling. The Google Mobile Ads SDK also declares the Advertising ID permission and Android Privacy Sandbox ad-services permissions, along with components used to deliver and measure ads.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone, SMS, or notification permissions.

5. Advertising and Consent (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. We do not use rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process limited information in connection with ad delivery, including:

  • Advertising ID and similar device or app identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information such as model, operating-system version, app version, and language
  • Ad interactions such as impressions, clicks, and view duration
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery and fraud prevention

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with applicable law. Where required, personalized advertising is based on your consent.

On first launch, and where required by law, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may present a consent form. If you decline personalized ads, ad requests are made in non-personalized mode. Non-personalized ads may still be shown and may use limited data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregate reporting.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad personalization → Manage consent. You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before you withdrew it.

Google may process ad-related data in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy. See policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising policies.

6. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party libraries and platforms:

  • Capacitor (by Ionic) — native runtime; no data collected by us
  • Ionic — UI framework; no data collected by us
  • i18next — localization; no data collected by us
  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform — see section 5 above

The app may open external links, such as the Google Play listing or Rockstar's end-user license agreement. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not maintain a server-side database of app users. Advertising-related data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy terms, as described in section 5.

8. Children's Privacy

GTA Cheats Guide is not directed to children under the age of 13. The Grand Theft Auto games are mature titles, and this guide is intended for their intended audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because your app data is stored locally, you have direct control over it:

  • Delete all app data: go to your device Settings → Apps → GTA Cheats Guide → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes all locally stored preferences and consent state.
  • Change ad consent: use Settings → Ad personalization → Manage consent inside the app.
  • Manage Google ad settings: use your Android Advertising ID controls or Google's ad settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. You may contact us using the details below. For data processed by Google as an advertising provider, please use Google's privacy controls; we can help direct your request where appropriate. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Security

The app has no backend database, and your preferences remain in the app's private storage on your device. Ad requests are handled by Google's SDK using encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to minimize the data the app handles.
